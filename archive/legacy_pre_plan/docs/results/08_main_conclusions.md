# Main Conclusions

- The atlas documents long-run descriptive patterns in EFW and macro indicators across quinquennial windows.
- Coverage and missingness are central to interpretation; early decades are thinner and reported explicitly.
- Cross-country heterogeneity remains large in levels, changes, and rank mobility.
- Co-movement plots show associations that are descriptive only and not causal.

Next steps
- Extend coverage diagnostics for additional indicators or alternative regional groupings.
- Add country or regional narrative summaries where data density permits.
